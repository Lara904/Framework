# NFR-6202 — The installation of developments is under the exclusive responsibility of technical integrators (concept of separating roles)

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6202
- **Title:** The installation of developments is under the exclusive responsibility of technical integrators (concept of separating roles)
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision aims to ensure the monitoring of programs used in production. It is particularly useful for research following an incident or a case of malpractice.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Cloud Context: Project team must be autonomous to code deploy in Dev or Staging environment, but the deployment in production must be limited to only a few people identified.
- For hotfixes or application minor/major version, deploy in production is not restricted to OPS, it could be executed by other people identified in the project team.
- Any infrastructure modification or technical modification must be managed and deployed by the OPS, SYSOPS.
- However, the process to deploy must be the same from the Dev environment to the production, only configuration must be changed.
- The Pipeline delivery must be automatic and manage by DEV & OPS close together. They guarantee of integrity, reliability, traceability and reproducibility of the process.
- Project team must limited to few people the access to deploy in production environment.
