# NFR-6004 — Access rights reviews

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6004
- **Title:** Access rights reviews
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision aims to ensure that there is no drift in time regarding attribution of rights This affects users and administrators. According to the information of the Guide of realization of the access rights reviews (PSA-GASSI_006), the PFA perform or have performed a review of the rights granted to users and administrators to its application; it sets the registration procedure of the record of the execution of this review of the rights and possible remedies.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This provision aims to ensure that there is no drift in time regarding attribution of rights This affects users and administrators.
- According to the information of the Guide of realization of the access rights reviews (PSA-GASSI_006), the PFA perform or have performed a review of the rights granted to users and administrators to its application; it sets the registration procedure of the record of the execution of this review of the rights and possible remedies.
- For administrator access of the DSIN staff, he relies upon the results from rights reviews performed by the relevant DSIN management (proprietors of the REUNIS access service required for these administrators).
