# NFR-3201 — IP services of the system are identified (map of flows)

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-3201
- **Title:** IP services of the system are identified (map of flows)
- **Primary Category:** Compatibility

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Details: Stream mapping: Inventory of IP service application. It is to know the network application exchanges, protocols used, port numbers, a sense of sharing.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Compatibility standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Stream mapping: Inventory of IP service application.
It is to know the network application exchanges, protocols used, port numbers, a sense of sharing.
This is important for agility at the opening of the application to the outside, information is the responsibility of the ICC or RIA, but it must be known technical integration teams.
