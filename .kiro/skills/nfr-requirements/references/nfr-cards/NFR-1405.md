# NFR-1405 — The exercise of the rights of the natural person on their personal data are specified

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-1405
- **Title:** The exercise of the rights of the natural person on their personal data are specified
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** In order to facilitate the exercise of his rights by a data subject (Person), you should implement the adequate technical or organisational features. The GDPR defines several rights to the data subject (Persons) so that they keep control on their data: Right to obtain information on his rights and on the processing done with his personal data Right of access by the data subject to his personal data processed (see Article 15 of GDPR)
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- In order to facilitate the exercise of his rights by a data subject (Person), you should implement the adequate technical or organisational features.

The GDPR defines several rights to the data subject (Persons) so that they keep control on their data:
- Right to obtain information on his rights and on the processing done with his personal data
- Right of access by the data subject to his personal data processed (see Article 15 of GDPR)
- Right to rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning him (see Article 16 of GDPR)
- Right to erasure or ‘right to be forgotten’ (see Article 17 of GDPR)
- Right to restriction of processing (see Article 18 of GDPR)
- Right to data portability (see Article 20 of GDPR)
- Right to object, in particular for direct marketing purposes (see Article 21 of GDPR)
