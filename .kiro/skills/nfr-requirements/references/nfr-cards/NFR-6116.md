# NFR-6116 — Data from client workstations is unreadable if the station is returned, stolen or lost

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6116
- **Title:** Data from client workstations is unreadable if the station is returned, stolen or lost
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision aims to protect the Group's historical information and personal data (respect of data privacy legislation) For PSA V3 Station the implementation is the use of a whole disk encryption solution, otherwise the implementation may consist of a physical destruction of the drive or "digital shredding" of the disc, produced by a succession of systematic disk writes; This operation is conducted by the "support the position" between the PC and replace the output PSA responsibility.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---
