# NFR-1406 — When intending to work with a non-EU supplier, the supplier must accept the EU legal obligations

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-1406
- **Title:** When intending to work with a non-EU supplier, the supplier must accept the EU legal obligations
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Choose the bidder that offers the right level of data privacy guarantees (for example the one that offers a certification/label or privacy enhancing tools). During a tender, evaluate the necessity to sign a stand-alone agreement for data privacy (data privacy agreement for instance) with each bidder concerned before choosing the winner.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Choose the bidder that offers the right level of data privacy guarantees (for example the one that offers a certification/label or privacy enhancing tools). 

During a tender, evaluate the necessity to sign a stand-alone agreement for data privacy (data privacy agreement for instance) with each bidder concerned before choosing the winner. Only if the level of risk for the data subject (Person) seems to be important. 

Before choosing a non-EU supplier, ensure that the supplier has signed the "Standard Contractual Clauses" (SCC) of the European Union.
