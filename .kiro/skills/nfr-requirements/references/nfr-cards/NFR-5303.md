# NFR-5303 — The technical environment (electricity, cooling, etc.) is secured with hardware redundancy

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5303
- **Title:** The technical environment (electricity, cooling, etc.) is secured with hardware redundancy
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Details: This provision aims to cover a breakdown in the technical environment in a service required for the correct operation of systems. The sites hosting the servers have implemented systems to ensure: Continuity of electricity supply (inverters, power generating units, etc.),
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This provision aims to cover a breakdown in the technical environment in a service required for the correct operation of systems.
The sites hosting the servers have implemented systems to ensure:
- Continuity of electricity supply (inverters, power generating units, etc.),
- The continuity of a functioning temperature that is compatible with server tolerances (back-up air conditioning, chilled water system, etc.).
- These provisions are operational in our consolidation centres.
- On Cloud platforms (AWS, AZURE, GCP …)
- The cloud provider is responsible of this item regarding the contract agreement and the SLA of each service used by Datacenter.
