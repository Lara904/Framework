# NFR-5403 — The operational application lead (POA) of the system has a range of functional tests to apply during a restart

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5403
- **Title:** The operational application lead (POA) of the system has a range of functional tests to apply during a restart
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** The range of functional tests is defined by the user; the CP-MOE must ensure its existence and relevance. It validates proper functioning of the application, after a stop / recovery for example and control the service restoration in case of DRP (PRA).
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- The range of functional tests is defined by the user; the CP-MOE must ensure its existence and relevance. It validates proper functioning of the application, after a stop / recovery for example and control the service restoration in case of DRP (PRA). 
Also applies to CFAO.

Realization:
- Realization of ranges by the CP-MOA
Validation of ranges by the CP-MOE
