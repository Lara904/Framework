# NFR-5204 — Maintenance contracts, with repair times tailored to DICT level, are up to date

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5204
- **Title:** Maintenance contracts, with repair times tailored to DICT level, are up to date
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision should allow the IT material to be brought up to level if it suffers a breakdown. To achieve this, a service provider is called, with whom time-scales for action and repair are the subject of a contract including terms that are coherent with the service level planned for the applications depending on this hardware.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This provision should allow the IT material to be brought up to level if it suffers a breakdown. To achieve this, a service provider is called, with whom time-scales for action and repair are the subject of a contract including terms that are coherent with the service level planned for the applications depending on this hardware.
- These time-scales for repair are not necessarily short, and depend, in particular, on the support level expected, opening hours, and the existence of technical or organisational security system (server farm system, cluster mode, degraded procedures, etc. ).

On Cloud platforms (AWS, AZURE, GCP …)
- On the cloud the maintenance contracts are driven by SLA: each service has its SLA. Regarding this SLA, the provider must restore the service if problem otherwise, it pay penalties.
