# NFR-6007 — Servers or proxies exposed on Internet should use certificates issued by certificate authorities known by default. Use of specific certification authority or specific installations should be avoid

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6007
- **Title:** Servers or proxies exposed on Internet should use certificates issued by certificate authorities known by default. Use of specific certification authority or specific installations should be avoid
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This non-functional requirement concerns servers or equipments that need to prove the authenticity of their domain name or recognize the legitimacy of their partners. They must then use known SSL certificates with their right certification chain.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This non-functional requirement concerns servers or equipments that need to prove the authenticity of their domain name or recognize the legitimacy of their partners. They must then use known SSL certificates with their right certification chain.
- For the connection to exposed servers on internet the certificate presented by the server or proxy can be issued by one of the 300 known certification authorities. It is not advisable to carry out a targeted check on a certification authority but rather to recognize all default certification authorities as do IE or Chrome browsers, which use Microsoft's Truststore.
- Another example using Java, it is necessary to keep the native Java behavior that relies on validating server certificates on its own truststore <java-home>/lib/security/cacerts. Overloading with an application truststore is not necessary. If the certificate authority used by the client is not recognized natively by java, it should be added in the native java truststore above.
- The creation of a specific truststore must remain an exception and must be done in full knowledge of the facts: this can be the case for example for the connection between machines in an intranet using a dedicated certification authority and specific procedures (installation, renewal).
- In case of doubt: cert-pub@mpsa.com
