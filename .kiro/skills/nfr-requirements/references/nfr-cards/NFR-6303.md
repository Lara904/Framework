# NFR-6303 — SOX Logs must be properly generated and retention included

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6303
- **Title:** SOX Logs must be properly generated and retention included
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Change log: Any modification for application code/database must be tracked into logs and into official change records into PSASM or ServiceNow. It is not allowed into SOX applications to do modifications without change records (with proper approval, test evidences, etc).
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).
