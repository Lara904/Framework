# NFR-6104 — Data backups and archives must have enhanced protection (encryption or safe)

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6104
- **Title:** Data backups and archives must have enhanced protection (encryption or safe)
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This is to prevent the risk of playing backed up or archived data. The implementation involves the use of encryption solution such data or a storage procedure in safes (physical or electronic).
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This is to prevent the risk of playing backed up or archived data.
The implementation involves the use of encryption solution such data or a storage procedure in safes (physical or electronic).
