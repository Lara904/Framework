# NFR-5203 — Needed on-call are operational

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5203
- **Title:** Needed on-call are operational
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Details: This provision should allow a suitable competent individual to be contacted at any moment, in order to establish a diagnosis and resolve an incident. This individual could be: business sector,
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This provision should allow a suitable competent individual to be contacted at any moment, in order to establish a diagnosis and resolve an incident. This individual could be:
- IT studies, in the case of a problem related to a program error,
- Technical integrator, in the case of a problem related to coordination of applications,
- Infrastructure, in the case of problems with network, system, etc.
- The implementation of this provision requires prior organisation (size of team, flexibility, involvement of Human Resources, etc.). By default, a Call/Reminder mechanism can meet requirements.
Reminder, the infrastructure teams have established an on-call system.
- On Cloud platforms (AWS, AZURE, GCP …)
- For cloud infrastructure, no Stellantis on-call is made. In case of infrastructure incident, the integrator in charge of the application must refer to cloud support.
- For applicative on-call: if needed, the same rule as on prem application must be applied:
- IT studies, in the case of a problem related to a program error,
- OPS, in the case of a problem related to the operation of applications.
