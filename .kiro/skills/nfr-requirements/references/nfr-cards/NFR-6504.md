# NFR-6504 — The owner of an access service must confirm access service assignations

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6504
- **Title:** The owner of an access service must confirm access service assignations
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Assigning rights to read/write for users/administrators with/without confirmation of the owner of REUNIS access service. This set of provisions aims to limit the risk of error by controlling, as well as possible, access according the population and level of rights open.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Assigning rights to read/write for users/administrators with/without confirmation of the owner of REUNIS access service.
This set of provisions aims to limit the risk of error by controlling, as well as possible, access according the population and level of rights open.
- Distinction reading/writing: providing reading only rights prevents risks of an attempt to harm the integrity.
- Distinction users/administrators: providing administrator access to a user constitutes an error (useless, and dangerous, access to information, which could harm the availability or integrity of the application, or allow knock on effects to other applications with administrator rights).
- Distinction with/without confirmation from proprietor: the proprietor of the access service is responsible for the usage that is made of the application functions made available through the access service. He chooses to rely upon the action of ASLs or to confirm each authorisation provided by an ASL. To direct the ASLs, he ensures the correct description of the access service in REUNIS.
