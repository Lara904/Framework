# NFR-4601 — The system meets users specific accessibility needs

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-4601
- **Title:** The system meets users specific accessibility needs
- **Primary Category:** Usability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Each population of users can have specific accessibility needs. The system must be designed to offer the good level of usability for all users.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Usability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- To identify specific accessibility needs for all kind of users, use the Populations and environments table. It allows to identify the different kind of populations the system will be designed for, and to identify the relevant characteristics for each of them and their environments.
- Then identify the adaptations to be made on the system:
- Describe in the functional specifications all impacts on the software design (specific user interfaces …),
- Describe in the technical architecture if specific hardware is needed (specific display, sound alerts, tactile device …).
