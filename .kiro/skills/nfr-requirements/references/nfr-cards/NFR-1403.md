# NFR-1403 — A complete Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) risk analysis on personal data has been conducted

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-1403
- **Title:** A complete Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) risk analysis on personal data has been conducted
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Conduct a risk analysis (see NFR-1401), when the risk is high (E, F) a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), must be done to analyse the risks of the new processing and determine the measures to be implemented to avoid and minimize the impacts. Useful links: Privacy by Design Guide
DPIA tool User Manual
A4 Privacy By Design
A4 DPIA
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).
