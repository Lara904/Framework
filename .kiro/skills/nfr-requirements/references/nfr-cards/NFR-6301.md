# NFR-6301 — Operational back-up and recovery mechanism for traceability data is implemented and tested

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6301
- **Title:** Operational back-up and recovery mechanism for traceability data is implemented and tested
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision aims to prevent the risk of loss over time of traceability data. Its implementation means that the data established in the "traceability" application module and the processing performed by this module are subject to backup and archiving processes tailored to their requirement for conservation established by the MOA; these processes are standard processes for infrastructure wherever possible, by default they are taken on by the application requestor.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).
