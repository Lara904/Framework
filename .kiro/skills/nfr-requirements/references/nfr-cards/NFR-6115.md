# NFR-6115 — Data extractions and downloads/upload (printing and others) must be controlled (technical and / or procedure)

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6115
- **Title:** Data extractions and downloads/upload (printing and others) must be controlled (technical and / or procedure)
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision aims to prevent careless spreading of printed information. Its implementation is based on a strong focus to raise awareness amongst users regarding the confidentiality of information.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Cloud context: define the rights according to the various actors of the application; a project manager will have more right than a simple average user. User management will be doing on a case-by-case basis, limiting the rights of users. We will use the TAM application as well as the rights on people in Ansible Tower to define who can do what.
- On AWS: S3 access must be logged.
