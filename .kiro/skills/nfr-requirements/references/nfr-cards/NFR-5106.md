# NFR-5106 — The change templates are up to date for each functional version

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5106
- **Title:** The change templates are up to date for each functional version
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** In order to put in production a functional version involving the respect of Impact on the BUSINESS and the “Defined” (DEF-J10ITE) Milestone between INDUS VCO and INDUS TN
2 Change templates must be written in PSASM: One for minor version without impact on the Business => Changes METIER type, One for major version, which involved or an impact on the BUSINESS or a “Defined” (DEF-J10ITE) milestone to be acted between INDUS teams.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- For each functional version:
- The change templates have to be created in the BIS change catalogue in PSASM.
They are under the PGA responsibility.
The project manager ask the PGA to choice the appropriate templates.

For each technical version:
- The change templates have to be created in the INDUS TN change catalogue in PSASM.
They are under the INDUS TN responsibility.
They are used by INDUS TN, in relation with INDUS VCO.
- On cloud context: AWS, AZURE or GCP:
- For minor version, a trace change must be generated automatically by pipeline delivery.
- For major version, regarding to its impact on others production applications, a trace change can be sufficient; otherwise, the internal team (CAB) who can estimate the business impact must be informed by this change (METIER). The Project Owner (IT-PM) must take care of the PSASM Charter.
- Useful links or documents:
- PSASM Change management Charter
- Cloud MAnagement IT_Changement as a service
