# NFR-6508 — Technical accounts are properly set-up in TAM

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6508
- **Title:** Technical accounts are properly set-up in TAM
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** All accounts in TAM must follow the below rules: - a password that follows the security rules - no goal as owner
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Applicate the rules that are described above for new and old accounts.
