# NFR-5102 — For hardware managed outside DDCE, the maintenance was forecasted and the critical hardware was identified

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5102
- **Title:** For hardware managed outside DDCE, the maintenance was forecasted and the critical hardware was identified
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Hardware Maintenance (MSTG responsibility) planned. Critical hardware identified (hardware with MSTG responsibility, hence outside disks, UC).
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Hardware Maintenance (MSTG responsibility) planned.
Critical hardware identified (hardware with MSTG responsibility, hence outside disks, UC).

Realization:
- Plan the troubleshooting procedure for problems in critical hardware (doubling or backup hardware; assess the volume of backup hardware with MSTG). The cost of backup hardware is a part of project cost;
- Communicate the list of critical hardware to MSTG, through GDC, so as to be able to prioritize its interventions ;
- Provide necessary and sufficient information to the site integrators for correct distribution of hardware of various concentrators in order to ensure the back-up ;
- Reference the critical hardware in the partnership contract when it exists.
- When the application is installed, the count is communicated to the site (GDC and/or MSTG) to update the Hardware repository (REFLEX) [existence of a ‘criticality’ indicator on the equipment name Id (Hostname)].
- In everyday life, during the meetings to organize partnership contracts, the list of critical hardware is rechecked and leads to update of CP and Hardware repository if necessary.
- In case of new hardware for the site, the CP-MOE should inform MSTG via GDC, so that he can set up training and ranges of installation for this hardware.
- Reminder: any hardware, critical or not, is identified in the Hardware repository. 

Examples:
- Critical hardware: for some critical applications, « bord de ligne » (production line) hardware (screens or printers), automates, barcode readers, …
