# NFR-6505 — The owner of a functional or technical administrator access service limits the number of administrators to the just/strictly necessary

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6505
- **Title:** The owner of a functional or technical administrator access service limits the number of administrators to the just/strictly necessary
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** This provision aims to limit the number of persons having privileged access. It aims to restrict the number of administrators to only groups of people in charge of this mission.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This provision aims to limit the number of persons having privileged access.
It aims to restrict the number of administrators to only groups of people in charge of this mission. Automatic systems adjusted to UNIX and Windows technologies have been established to apply this provision.
- The just necessary corresponds to the group of people in the DDCE teams without reason to control the DSIN operational modes.
- The strictly necessary corresponds to a number of around fifty persons, who may be involved in altering the DDCE operational modes, which could have an effect on the reactivity of actions. Only administrators explicitly in charge of actions to perform must be authorized.
