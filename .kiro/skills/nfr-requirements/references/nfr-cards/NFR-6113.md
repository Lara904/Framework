# NFR-6113 — The DICT of any third party application that use system's data must be consistent with the DICT of the system

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6113
- **Title:** The DICT of any third party application that use system's data must be consistent with the DICT of the system
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** The actions to be taken are the inventory of data-intensive applications the application. Raise in REFLEX DICT actually covered by the means established by the hungry applications and address the deviations from the DICT of the application.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- The actions to be taken are the inventory of data-intensive applications the application. Raise in REFLEX DICT actually covered by the means established by the hungry applications and address the deviations from the DICT of the application. When there is a gap potentially offers 3 possibilities: 1) Contingent DICT Reassessment of the consuming application and implementation of adequate resources; 2) assumption of risk by the owner of the data of the application "consumed"; 3 Removal of trade.
