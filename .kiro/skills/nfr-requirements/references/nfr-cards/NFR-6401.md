# NFR-6401 — Control procedures of the deviant behaviours are defined and implemented

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6401
- **Title:** Control procedures of the deviant behaviours are defined and implemented
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Define the "use case" for deviant behavior research (example fraud). Define the data needed for registration for good control of deviant behaviour This requires defining behaviour patterns and / or behaviours standards and monitor compliance of the activity in relation to the inventory.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Define the "use case" for deviant behavior research (example fraud).
- Define the data needed for registration for good control of deviant behaviour
- This requires defining behaviour patterns and / or behaviours standards and monitor compliance of the activity in relation to the inventory. A good knowledge of the use of the application and ecosystem exploitation is necessary. The focus is often iterative. Behaviour control can be based for example on activity thresholds, time slots for access, access to different information is usually not covered by the same person, etc... Of course these controls rely on an action logging.
