# NFR-4501 — The system meets standard ergonomic rules and business graphic charters

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-4501
- **Title:** The system meets standard ergonomic rules and business graphic charters
- **Primary Category:** Usability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** The user interface is the contact between the users and the system, so it is very important to have a good design. We can distinguish two aspects here: The standard ergonomic rules,
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Usability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- This user interface design is done in the functional specifications, and also with mock-ups.
- There are 2 important steps here:
- The organizations of user’s tasks,
- The graphical interfaces.
- About the tasks organization, it is very important to understand who will use the system (identify the roles) and what users will have to do with it (identify the goals). For more information about how to do this, with UML or not, see the e-Learning about system analysis below.
- To work on graphical interfaces, the usage of a tool is very useful. You can use Justinmind Prototyper, available in the PSAv3.
