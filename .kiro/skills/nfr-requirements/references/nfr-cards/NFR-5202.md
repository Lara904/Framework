# NFR-5202 — Degraded processes exist and are tested (IT or/and organizational)

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-5202
- **Title:** Degraded processes exist and are tested (IT or/and organizational)
- **Primary Category:** Reliability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** The purpose of this provision is to guarantee a certain continuity of functioning of the service, for a limited period, to respond to a total or partial unavailability of the information system. This shall be established either through organisational processes, or by an additional information system (fax, telephone, etc.).
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Reliability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).
