# NFR-1407 — The personal data sent to a subcontractor for developments or tests purpose are anonymized or pseudonymized

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-1407
- **Title:** The personal data sent to a subcontractor for developments or tests purpose are anonymized or pseudonymized
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Make sure that the data sent to a subcontractor for development or testing are anonymized or pseudonymised. When a real risk for data subject (Person) is proven for a processing and when data anonymization is not possible, a way of reducing the risk for data subject (Person) is the pseudonymization.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Make sure that the data sent to a subcontractor for development or testing are anonymized or pseudonymised.

When a real risk for data subject (Person) is proven for a processing and when data anonymization is not possible, a way of reducing the risk for data subject (Person) is the pseudonymization.

Usually, anonymization is recommended in non-production environments to ensure the protection of personal data. In production, pseudonymization (combined with other practices: access restriction, minimization …) can help to protect personal data.

If real data is mandatory, a data protection agreement must be signed

Definitions:
- Anonymization is the technical process used to render impossible the re-identification of a data subject through available data. In order to achieve proper anonymization, the process should be irreversible in such a way that the data subject (Person) can no longer be isolated as an individual, their identity cannot be inferred from available data, and the data that relates to them can no longer be connected to them.
  
Pseudonymization is the technical process that consists in replacing any data for a data subject (Person) by a pseudonym in order to hide their identity. Usually only the data subject’s identifiers as name, email, social security number, VIN… are pseudonymized. Resorting to this method renders impossible the re-identification of a person as long as further information is not provided. Unlike anonymization, the process is reversible when the token is not deleted (if the token is deleted, this is an irreversible pseudonymization).
