# NFR-2302 — The increase of resource needs related to the increase of the business must be anticipated

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-2302
- **Title:** The increase of resource needs related to the increase of the business must be anticipated
- **Primary Category:** Performance Efficiency

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Before all GOPROD or main data increase, the Project Manager or the PGA, must: with the INDUS team: validate the capability of the infrastructure to accept the new work load (for example: CPU, Memory, volume, With the performance team and the INDUS team. They must test the reliability of the new queries.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Performance Efficiency standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Under INDUS TEAM control for CPU, volume and memory.

To check the performance of the new queries involved in the new version, please contact your INDUS TEAM and the Performance Team to put under monitoring the new queries.
