# NFR-6506 — The number of users having access to an information asset must be less than 150

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6506
- **Title:** The number of users having access to an information asset must be less than 150
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** The Group standard (PSA-STSSI_003) has fixed a maximum figure of around 150 users for an IT asset with a maximum level of confidentiality. Useful links: Standard risks reduction measures
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---
