# NFR-1202 — The system's scheduling entities produce consistent, complete results

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-1202
- **Title:** The system's scheduling entities produce consistent, complete results
- **Primary Category:** Functional Suitability

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** A planning entity is a sequence of processing. It corresponds to: z/OS: a TWS occurrence; UNIX, VMS and Windows: a $UNIVERSE session;
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Functional Suitability standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- A planning entity is a sequence of processing. 
It corresponds to:
- z/OS: a TWS occurrence;
- UNIX, VMS and Windows: a $UNIVERSE session;
- UNIX (hors $U): a line of crontab;
- Windows: a Task Scheduler task;
- Tandem: a Netbatch chain.
- Cloud context: Use a dedicated container never plan a crontab in container
- The provided results guarantee the proper and complete execution of the application (editions and transmissions). A planning entity should not provide partial results for incidents. 
Only when the planning entity completes correctly, that its results are available for the downstream applications or the user.

Realization:
Regroup the publishing and sending of results at the end of planning entity, after the availability, which is the point of « non-retour » (no-return) for the planning entity.
- On Cloud platforms (AWS, AZURE, GCP …)
- Better use cloud provider triggers to start/stop containers or VM.
