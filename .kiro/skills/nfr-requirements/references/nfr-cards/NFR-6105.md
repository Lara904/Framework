# NFR-6105 — Protection must be enhanced when data backups or archives are stored by a third party

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6105
- **Title:** Protection must be enhanced when data backups or archives are stored by a third party
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Data Encryption is necessary for external storage (Cloud, vaulting,…)

Realization:
On premise: Source encryption before backup/copy/archive and externalization, 
Or Target encryption with best solution regarding the technology. Contact infrastructure team (Storage, backup) to define the best solution depending of the application.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Only for C2 or C3 data (Remind: C4 must not be outside).
