# NFR-6402 — The traceability chart corresponding to business's traceability requirements (which are mandatory) is defined

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-6402
- **Title:** The traceability chart corresponding to business's traceability requirements (which are mandatory) is defined
- **Primary Category:** Security

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** Traceability needs defined by the business for the actions of users and administrators (content, shelf life, publication and analysis of traceability data). Create a synthetic description of the traceability provisions implemented to respond to the requirements expressed by the business.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Security standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- Traceability needs defined by the business for the actions of users and administrators (content, shelf life, publication and analysis of traceability data).
Create a synthetic description of the traceability provisions implemented to respond to the requirements expressed by the business.

The types of information to be retained are, in particular, depending on the DICT application values:
- when the C> = 3 it will take for example keep track of read access sensitive information,
- when there is an I> = 3 it will be relevant to record the actions of change,
- when one of the criteria D, I or C> = 3 then it is important to trace the administrative actions of security,
- When the system contains auditable data, any access to sensitive data (e.g.: financial or personal data, accounting or management system, style, product plan, etc.), be it a read and/or modification, should be recovered later if an audit is necessary. It should guarantee information accuracy. The actions that cause modifications in content should be traced based on evaluation of T in DICT.
