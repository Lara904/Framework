# NFR-2102 — The users requirements about the system response times are validated with the business and tested

## 1. NFR Metadata

- **ID:** NFR-2102
- **Title:** The users requirements about the system response times are validated with the business and tested
- **Primary Category:** Performance Efficiency

---

## 2. Executive Summary

- **The "Goal":** The IT-PM and the ART must define the good technical architecture for the system. One important input is the performances required by the users, associated with the anticipated data volumes the system will have to manage.
- **Why it matters:** Failure to implement this NFR may result in non-compliance with Performance Efficiency standards, increased operational risk, and potential rejection during technical control gates (TCC/CTC).

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

### Key Constraints

- All function of the system, including screens and batches, are described in the functional specifications. The needs about response times must be written at the good level inside this documentation:
- At the function level if the response time is specific to that function,
- At a higher level if it concerns a group of functions.
- If UML and Enterprise Architect are used, the response times are indicated in the description of use-cases and activities used to describe the business goals and function, or even at a higher level, in packages description, if needed.

Answer **Yes** to any of the following to confirm this NFR applies to your project:
